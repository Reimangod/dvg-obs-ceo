# PRA critical path S4 prior-art result

Decision: `GO_S5_NOVELTY_SCOPE_DEFENSIBLE`

This stage is a bounded primary-source audit, not a proof that no related work
exists anywhere. Ten directly inspected works are recorded with stable
identifiers: eight peer-reviewed articles and two 2026 preprints. Publication
status is never merged.

## Direct prior art

The following elements cannot be claimed as new:

- CEO operators, MVP/OVP definitions, or native CEO circuits;
- shared-parameter CEO sum/difference families;
- generic parameter or operator pruning;
- QFI/effective-dimension redundancy removal;
- CNOT-efficient excitation synthesis;
- full-ansatz resource comparison.

In particular, the CEO paper is direct prior art for coupled-exchange blocks,
the MVP/OVP distinction, shared-parameter CEO families, and native
CNOT-efficient implementations. Pruned-ADAPT-VQE, PECT, dimensional
expressivity, and quantum-geometry methods establish broad pruning and
parameter-redundancy precedent.

## Defensible distinction

No source in the bounded audit was found to report the same combined protocol:

> A synthesis-certified finite registered rank-demotion framework for
> three-constituent MVP-CEO blocks, with target-native resynthesis and
> orthogonal semantic, optimization, transaction, and resource evidence.

This wording is deliberately narrower than claiming that a generic
three-to-two transition, parameter tying, pruning, or excitation synthesis is
new. Until the remaining evidence program is complete, the frozen safe claim
is:

> a synthesis-certified registered rank demotion not present in the compared
> V4.1 implementation.

## Claim boundary

The audit does not authorize a matched-work or cross-molecule performance
claim. S5 may perform only an outcome-free structural applicability census.
Candidate energy and all other performance outcomes remain unauthorized until
the S6 gate.

The machine-readable artifact stores every source, status, identifier, feature
classification, search family, novelty-matrix entry, and prohibited claim.
